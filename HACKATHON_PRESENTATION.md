# PRESENTATION TIPS

## Delivery Guidelines:
1. **Start with the problem** - Hook judges with the $10B stat
2. **Show, don't tell** - Use live demo early (Slide 5)
3. **Address the obvious** - "Why not just use Grammarly?" (Slide 10)
4. **End with action** - QR code for immediate testing
5. **Time allocation:**
   - Problem/Solution: 2 min
   - Live Demo: 2 min
   - Tech/Market: 2 min
   - Traction/Ask: 2 min
   - Q&A: 2 min

## What Judges Care About:
- ✅ **Technical complexity** - Show the 3D landing page, mention multi-model AI
- ✅ **Real problem** - Use data ($10B market, 60% violation rate)
- ✅ **Working product** - Live demo is your biggest asset
- ✅ **Business model** - Clear pricing, revenue projections
- ✅ **Scalability** - Serverless architecture, API-first design

## Common Questions & Answers:

**Q: "How is this different from Grammarly?"**
A: "Grammarly checks grammar and spelling. We check brand. Custom rules like 'never say click here' or 'always mention our mission.' It's apples and oranges."

**Q: "What if rules conflict?"**
A: "We have severity levels. Errors block publishing, warnings suggest improvements. Users can override warnings with justification—tracked in analytics."

**Q: "How do you prevent false positives?"**
A: "Our engine is deterministic, not AI-based. It matches exact phrases you define. If it flags something, it's because your rule says so. Full transparency."

**Q: "Can it integrate with our CMS?"**
A: "Yes! API-first architecture. We have webhooks and REST endpoints. Already planning Slack, Teams, Figma integrations. CMS integration is next."

**Q: "What about privacy/security?"**
A: "Content stays in your tenant. No training on customer data. SOC 2 compliance roadmap for enterprise. Open to on-premise deployment for sensitive industries."

**Q: "How do you compete with enterprise DAMs?"**
A: "We don't. We integrate. DAM stores files, we validate content. Think of us as the 'brand spell-checker' layer on top of existing tools."

**Q: "Pricing seems low for enterprise..."**
A: "Land-and-expand. Start with one team at $199/mo, expand to enterprise custom pricing ($50K+/year). Average customer LTV is $50K over 3 years."
